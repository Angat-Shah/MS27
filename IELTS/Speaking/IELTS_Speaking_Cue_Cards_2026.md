---

## Executive Summary & Test Structure

The **IELTS Speaking Test** is a face-to-face interview evaluating verbal communication across four core criteria: **Fluency & Coherence (25%)**, **Lexical Resource (25%)**, **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (25%)**, and **Pronunciation (25%)**.

| Test Part | Duration | Format & Task Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Part 1** | 4–5 mins | Introduction and personal questions on familiar topics (work, study, hobbies, hometown). |
| **Part 2** | 3–4 mins | **Cue Card Presentation**: 1 minute preparation time, followed by a 1 to 2-minute speech based on a task card. |
| **Part 3** | 4–5 mins | **Two-Way Discussion**: In-depth analytical follow-up questions linked to the Part 2 topic. |
