## Report

The provided tabular illustration compares social and economic parameters for various countries using four different indicators according to United Nations statistics in 1994.

Overall, Japan and Canada consistently outperformed Peru and Zaire across all four indicators. Japan recorded the highest annual income and the longest life expectancy, while Canada had the greatest daily calorie supply. In contrast, Zaire ranked lowest in every category.

Regarding annual income, Japan had the highest income per person at US$15,760, followed by Canada with US$11,100. By comparison, Peru and Zaire reported much lower figures of just US$160 and US$130, respectively. A similar pattern can be observed in life expectancy, with people in Japan expected to live for 78 years and those in Canada for 76 years, compared with 51 years in Peru and 47 years in Zaire.

The remaining indicators show the same trend. Canada had the highest daily calorie supply, at 3,326 calories per person, slightly ahead of Japan with 2,846 calories. Peru and Zaire recorded considerably lower figures of 1,927 and 1,749 calories, respectively. Likewise, adult literacy stood at 99% in both developed countries, compared with 68% in Peru and just 34% in Zaire.
