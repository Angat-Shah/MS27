# IELTS T2|07 – Childhood Obesity & Fitness

**“In many countries, children are getting fatter and less fit day by day. Why is it so? Suggest solutions.”**

Word Count: **263**

---

## Essay

Around the world, the number of overweight and physically unfit children has increased significantly in recent years. This trend is mainly caused by unhealthy lifestyles and modern technology. However, it can be addressed through the combined efforts of families, schools, and governments.

One compelling reason why children are becoming obese and less fit is the growing habit of engaging in sedentary activities. Many young people spend several hours using smartphones, playing video games, or watching television instead of participating in outdoor sports or physical exercise. Consequently, they burn fewer calories and gradually develop unhealthy lifestyles. To address this issue, schools should provide regular physical education classes and organize sports competitions to encourage students to be more active. Parents should also set limits on screen time and motivate their children to engage in outdoor activities.

Another significant reason is the increasing consumption of unhealthy food. Fast food, aerated drinks, and processed snacks are widely available, affordable, and heavily advertised, making them more attractive than nutritious meals. In addition, busy parents often rely on convenience foods because they have limited time to prepare healthy home-cooked meals. A practical solution is to educate both children and parents about the importance of a balanced diet. Furthermore, governments should regulate advertisements promoting unhealthy foods to children and support healthier eating by raising public awareness.

In conclusion, the increasing rate of obesity and poor fitness among youth is mainly the result of inactive lifestyles and unhealthy diets. By promoting activity, improving nutrition education, and creating a healthier environment, society can help children develop better habits and enjoy healthier futures.
