## Task 1 – Australian Water Consumption

**“The bar chart below shows the average Australian water consumption in selected cities for last year. The pie chart shows the distribution of Australian water consumption for last year. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.”**

Word Count: **203**
Score: **2.0**

---

### Report

The bar chart illustrates the amount of water consumed per property in selected Australian cities during the previous year, while the pie chart shows how water consumption was distributed across different sectors in Australia in 2014.

Overall, water consumption varied considerably among the cities. Perth recorded by far the highest level of water usage, whereas Canberra had the lowest. Regarding sectoral consumption, agriculture accounted for the largest proportion of water use nationwide, while the remaining sectors made up significantly smaller shares.

According to the bar chart, Perth consumed approximately 300 kilolitres of water per property, compared with only about 130 kilolitres in Canberra. The figures for other cities, such as Sydney, Adelaide, Brisbane, and Melbourne, ranged from around 150 to 250 kilolitres per property. Although there were some differences among these cities, the variation was relatively modest compared with the gap between Perth and Canberra.

As shown in the pie chart, agriculture was responsible for the largest share of Australia's total water consumption, accounting for 67%. Households represented the second-largest proportion at 9%, followed by both water services and utility services, each contributing 7%. By contrast, the commercial sector and other industries consumed only relatively small proportions of the nation's total water use.

## Task 2 – Medical Science Breakthroughs

**“The breakthroughs in medical science are by far the most significant advances in world society over the last two centuries. Discuss this statement and give your opinion.”**

Word Count: **291**
Score: **4.0**

---

### Essay

Medical science has transformed human life over the past two centuries through remarkable advances such as vaccines, antibiotics, organ transplantation, and modern diagnostic technologies. While some people argue that these breakthroughs represent the greatest progress in modern history, others believe that developments in fields such as technology, transportation, and communication have been equally influential. I partially agree that medical achievements have had the most profound impact because they have fundamentally improved both the quality and length of human life.

The primary reason medical science stands above other advances is its direct contribution to reducing mortality and increasing life expectancy. Before the discovery of antibiotics and vaccines, millions of people died from diseases that are now easily prevented or treated. For instance, the eradication of smallpox and the near elimination of polio have saved countless lives worldwide. In addition, innovations such as MRI scanners and advanced cancer treatments enable doctors to diagnose illness earlier and provide more effective care. Subsequently, people today not only live longer but also enjoy healthier and more productive lives.

However, it would be an oversimplification not to acknowledge the importance of other sectors. The development of the internet and digital communication has revolutionized education, business, and international collaboration, while advances in transportation have connected economies and cultures on an unprecedented scale. Furthermore, many medical innovations themselves rely on breakthroughs in computing, artificial intelligence, and engineering. These fields therefore complement rather than compete with medical science.

In conclusion, although technological and industrial development have undoubtedly reshaped society, I believe that medical science has produced the greatest overall benefit. By protecting people from disease, extending life expectancy, and enhancing quality of life, medical breakthroughs have laid the foundation upon which many other social and economic achievements have been built.
