# IELTS T1|12 – Music Format Profits in Europe

## Report

The bar charts compare the profits generated by SMG Entertainment from five distinct music formats across Germany, France, and Italy in 1997 and 2007, measured in millions of euros.

Overall, physical CDs purchased in retail shops remained the primary revenue driver across all three nations, despite experiencing a considerable downturn over the decade. Concurrently, digital formats—namely online CD orders and internet downloads—emerged as growing income sources, whereas older analog mediums like cassettes and vinyl records became completely obsolete.

In 1997, in-store CD sales generated the highest earnings by a wide margin, yielding approximately €42 million in Germany, €25 million in France, and €20 million in Italy. In comparison, earnings from other formats were negligible: cassette sales contributed between €5 million and €7 million across the three countries, vinyl records produced only €1 to €3 million, and digital downloads were completely non-existent.

By 2007, profits from physical shop-bought CDs fell markedly to €25 million in Germany, €15 million in France, and €10 million in Italy. However, this drop was partially offset by digital gains, with online CD sales rising significantly in Germany and France, and digital downloads reaching around €5 million in each. Conversely, cassettes and vinyl formats ceased generating any revenue.
