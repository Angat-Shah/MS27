## Report

The diagram illustrates the process of producing canned fruit, beginning with harvesting and ending with distribution to customers.

Overall, the procedure consists of fourteen stages, starting with hand-picking fresh fruit and finishing with the dispatch of the final canned product. Before the fruit is processed, it undergoes several preparation and quality-control stages to ensure that only suitable produce is used.

Initially, ripe fruit is harvested by hand and transported to the processing facility, where it is thoroughly washed. It is then inspected for quality, after which the selected fruit is placed in cold storage. The stored fruit is subsequently weighed and graded according to its size and quality before being peeled. Once the outer skin has been removed, the fruit is cored and sliced into appropriate pieces.

Furthermore, the prepared fruit is placed into cans. These cans are then sealed tightly to prevent contamination before being cooked and sterilized at a high temperature. After the sterilization process has been completed, labels are attached to the cans, which are then transferred to a storage warehouse. Finally, the finished products are dispatched to retailers or other destinations for sale and consumption.
