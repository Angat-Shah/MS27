# IELTS T2|01 – Natural Resources

**“The natural resources such as oil and fresh water are being consumed at an alarming rate. What problems does it cause? How can we solve this problem?”**

Word Count: **254**

---

## Essay

Natural resources such as oil and fresh water are essential for human survival and economic development. However, these resources are being consumed at an alarming rate due to population growth, industrialization, and excessive consumption. This trend creates serious environmental and economic problems, but several effective measures can help address the issue.

One of the most significant consequences is environmental degradation. The excessive use of fossil fuels increases greenhouse gas emissions, leading to global warming and climate change. As a result, extreme weather events, rising sea levels, and loss of biodiversity are becoming more common. In addition, the overuse of fresh water causes water shortages, making it difficult for people to meet their daily needs. Agriculture and industries also suffer when there is a scarcity of water supplies, which can threaten food production and economic stability.

To tackle these problems, both governments and individuals must take responsibility. Firstly, countries should invest in renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power to reduce dependence on oil. Governments can also introduce stricter environmental regulations and encourage energy-efficient technologies. Secondly, fresh water should be used more responsibly by promoting water-saving practices, repairing leaking infrastructure, and recycling wastewater. Public awareness campaigns can educate people about conserving natural resources in their daily lives.

In conclusion, the rapid depletion of oil and fresh water has serious consequences for the environment, the economy, and future generations. By adopting sustainable energy sources, conserving water, and encouraging responsible consumption, society can protect these valuable resources and ensure a healthier future for everyone.
