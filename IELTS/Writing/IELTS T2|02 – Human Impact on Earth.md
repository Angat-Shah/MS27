# IELTS T2|02 – Human Impact on Earth

**“Some people say we humans are harming the Earth through our activities, but other people say we are improving the quality of life on our planet. Which opinion do you agree with?”**

Word Count: **276**

---

## Essay

Whether human activities are ultimately beneficial or deleterious remains a subject of considerable debate. While human innovation has transformed living standards through advances in science and technology, it has also imposed unprecedented pressure on the natural environment. Although both sides present valid arguments, I believe that the environmental costs of unchecked development have become too significant to ignore.

On the one hand, human progress has greatly enhanced the quality of life. Breakthroughs in medicine, transportation, and communication have enabled people to live longer, healthier, and more connected lives. A clear example is the rapid development of COVID-19 vaccines, which saved millions of lives and demonstrated how scientific collaboration can address global crises. Similarly, agricultural innovation has improved food quality and reduced hunger in many developing countries. These achievements illustrate that human ingenuity, when directed responsibly, has the potential to improve society on a remarkable scale.

On the other hand, many of these advancements have come at the expense of the planet. Rapid industrialization, deforestation, and the excessive use of fossil fuels have accelerated climate change, polluted ecosystems, and depleted natural resources. For instance, large-scale deforestation in the Amazon rainforest has destroyed wildlife habitats and reduced the Earth’s capacity to absorb carbon dioxide. Although technological progress has generated immense benefits, these gains will prove unsustainable unless governments, industries, and individuals adopt more environmentally responsible practices.

In conclusion, I acknowledge that human activities have significantly improved people’s lives, yet they have also created serious environmental challenges. Therefore, I believe the focus should not be on choosing between development and conservation, but on ensuring that future progress is guided by the principles of sustainability and responsible stewardship.
