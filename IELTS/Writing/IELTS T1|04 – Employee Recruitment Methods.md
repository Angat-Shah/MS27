## Report

The provided diagram illustrates three different methods used to recruit new employees: advertising vacancies within the company, advertising them in the press, and using an external recruitment agency. Overall, while all three methods aim to appoint suitable staff, they differ in the source of the applicants and the stages involved in the recruitment process.

The first method is internal recruitment. A vacancy is advertised inside the company so that existing employees can apply for promotion or transfer. Applications are shortlisted, suitable candidates are interviewed, and the most qualified employee is offered the job.

The second approach involves placing an advertisement in the press to attract external applicants. Interested candidates submit their applications, after which they are shortlisted and invited for interviews and testing. Following the selection process, the most suitable applicant is provided with the job offer.

The final method uses an external recruitment agency. The agency first advertises the vacancy and also promotes it within the company to identify potential candidates. It then screens applications before recommending suitable applicants to the employer for interviews. Compared with other methods, this approach reduces the employer’s initial workload, whereas press advertising generally reaches the widest range of potential candidates.
