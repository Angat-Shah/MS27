## Report

The provided diagram illustrates the process by which rainwater is collected, treated, and reused for domestic purposes.

Overall, the system is a sustainable cycle in which rainwater is gathered from different sources, purified for household use, and later processed again for recycling. This process reduces water wastage while ensuring a continuous supply of clean water.

Initially, rainwater falling in the mountains is stored in a dam before being transported to a water treatment plant. After purification, it is supplied to households as drinking water. At the same time, rainwater collected from house roofs is stored in a rainwater tank and directed into homes for various domestic activities.

Once the water has been used, it becomes household wastewater and is sent to a wastewater treatment plant. There, it undergoes further treatment before being recycled and returned to homes for non-drinking purposes. Finally, any excess treated water is safely discharged into the environment. Ultimately, the closed-loop system promotes efficient water conservation by maximizing the reuse of rainwater while maintaining a dependable supply of clean water.
