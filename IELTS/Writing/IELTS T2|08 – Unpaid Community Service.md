# IELTS T2|08 – Unpaid Community Service

**“It is suggested that all young adults should undertake a period of unpaid work, helping people in the community. Does it bring more benefits or drawbacks to the young people?”**

Word Count: **290**

---

## Essay

Some people argue that all young adults should devote a period of their lives to unpaid community service in order to support local society. Although such a requirement may create certain financial and practical difficulties, I firmly believe its positive impact on individuals and communities is far greater.

The most compelling advantage is that community service equips young adults with practical skills that are difficult to acquire in a classroom. Working with children, elderly people, or disadvantaged groups develops communication, teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving abilities through real-life scenarios. In addition, volunteering encourages independence, responsibility, and adaptability, all of which are essential for success in both personal and professional life. As a result, participants become more mature and better prepared for future challenges.

Another significant benefit is that unpaid community work strengthens social awareness while improving future career opportunities. By interacting with people from different backgrounds, young adults develop empathy and gain a deeper understanding of the challenges faced by their communities. Furthermore, employers increasingly appreciate applicants with volunteer experience because it reflects initiative, commitment, and a willingness to contribute beyond personal interests.

Admittedly, compulsory unpaid work may place financial pressure on individuals who rely on paid employment, and some participants may feel less motivated if they are forced to volunteer. There is also a possibility that some organizations could exploit unpaid workers. However, these concerns can be addressed through sensible measures such as providing travel allowances, offering exemptions for those facing financial hardship, and limiting the duration of the service.

In conclusion, although compulsory community service may present some economic and motivational challenges, its role in developing practical skills, enhancing employability, and fostering social responsibility makes it a highly valuable initiative. For these reasons, the advantages clearly outweigh the disadvantages.
