### Question 1
Points $R$, $S$, and $T$ lie on a number line, where $S$ is between $R$ and $T$.  
The distance between $R$ and $S$ is $6$, and the distance between $R$ and $T$ is $15$.

| Quantity A | Quantity B |
| :---: | :---: |
| The distance between the midpoints of line segments $RS$ and $ST$ | The distance between $S$ and $T$ |

- [A] Quantity A is greater.
- [B] Quantity B is greater.
- [C] The two quantities are equal.
- [D] The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[B] Quantity B is greater.**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. Since $S$ lies between $R$ and $T$ on a straight line:
>    $$\text{Distance}(R, T) = \text{Distance}(R, S) + \text{Distance}(S, T)$$
>    $$15 = 6 + \text{Distance}(S, T) \implies \text{Distance}(S, T) = 9$$
>    Thus, **Quantity B = 9**.
> 2. The midpoint of segment $RS$ is at distance $\frac{6}{2} = 3$ from $S$ (towards $R$).
> 3. The midpoint of segment $ST$ is at distance $\frac{9}{2} = 4.5$ from $S$ (towards $T$).
> 4. Since the two midpoints lie on opposite sides of point $S$, the distance between them is:
>    $$\text{Distance} = 3 + 4.5 = 7.5$$
>    Thus, **Quantity A = 7.5**.
> 5. Comparing quantities: $7.5 < 9$, so **Quantity B is greater**.

</details>

### Question 3
The length of each side of rectangle $R$ is an integer, and the area of $R$ is $36$.

| Quantity A | Quantity B |
| :---: | :---: |
| The number of possible values of the perimeter of $R$ | $6$ |

- [A] Quantity A is greater.
- [B] Quantity B is greater.
- [C] The two quantities are equal.
- [D] The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[B] Quantity B is greater.**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. The area of rectangle $R$ is $\text{length} \times \text{width} = 36$. Since side lengths are positive integers, find all unique integer factor pairs of 36:
>    * $1 \times 36 \implies \text{Perimeter} = 2(1 + 36) = 74$
>    * $2 \times 18 \implies \text{Perimeter} = 2(2 + 18) = 40$
>    * $3 \times 12 \implies \text{Perimeter} = 2(3 + 12) = 30$
>    * $4 \times 9 \implies \text{Perimeter} = 2(4 + 9) = 26$
>    * $6 \times 6 \implies \text{Perimeter} = 2(6 + 6) = 24$ (A square is a rectangle).
> 2. There are exactly **5 distinct possible values** for the perimeter.
> 3. Quantity A $= 5$, Quantity B $= 6$. Since $5 < 6$, **Quantity B is greater**.

</details>

$x$, $y$, and $z$ are the lengths of the sides of a triangle.

<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

>    $$x + y + z > z + z = 2z$$

$n$ is a positive integer that is greater than $3$ and has $d$ positive divisors.

### Question 8
$m = 10^{32} + 2$. When $m$ is divided by $11$, the remainder is $r$.

| Quantity A | Quantity B |
| :---: | :---: |
| $r$ | $3$ |

- [A] Quantity A is greater.
- [B] Quantity B is greater.
- [C] The two quantities are equal.
- [D] The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[C] The two quantities are equal.**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. Use modular arithmetic modulo $11$:
>    $$10 \equiv -1 \pmod{11}$$
> 2. Raise both sides to the 32nd power:
>    $$10^{32} \equiv (-1)^{32} \equiv 1 \pmod{11}$$
> 3. Add 2:
>    $$m = 10^{32} + 2 \equiv 1 + 2 \equiv 3 \pmod{11}$$
> 4. Since $0 \le 3 < 11$, the remainder $r$ is exactly $3$.
> 5. Quantity A $= 3$ and Quantity B $= 3$. **The two quantities are equal**.

</details>

### Question 9
$xy = 8$ and $x = y - 2$.

| Quantity A | Quantity B |
| :---: | :---: |
| $x$ | $-4$ |

- [A] Quantity A is greater.
- [B] Quantity B is greater.
- [C] The two quantities are equal.
- [D] The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[D] The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. Substitute $x = y - 2$ into $xy = 8$:
>    $$(y - 2)y = 8 \implies y^2 - 2y - 8 = 0$$
> 2. Factor the quadratic equation:
>    $$(y - 4)(y + 2) = 0 \implies y = 4 \quad \text{or} \quad y = -2$$
> 3. Case 1: If $y = 4$, then $x = 4 - 2 = 2$.
>    * Quantity A $= 2$, Quantity B $= -4 \implies$ Quantity A > Quantity B.
> 4. Case 2: If $y = -2$, then $x = -2 - 2 = -4$.
>    * Quantity A $= -4$, Quantity B $= -4 \implies$ Quantity A = Quantity B.
> 5. Since Quantity A can be greater than or equal to Quantity B, the relationship **cannot be determined**.

</details>

### Question 10
The area of circle $W$ is $16\pi$ and the area of circle $Z$ is $4\pi$. What is the ratio of the circumference of $W$ to the circumference of $Z$?
- [A] $2$ to $1$
- [B] $4$ to $1$
- [C] $8$ to $1$
- [D] $16$ to $1$
- [E] $32$ to $1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[A] 2 to 1**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. The area of a circle is $\pi r^2$:
>    * For circle $W$: $\pi r_W^2 = 16\pi \implies r_W = 4$.
>    * For circle $Z$: $\pi r_Z^2 = 4\pi \implies r_Z = 2$.
> 2. The circumference of a circle is $2\pi r$. The ratio of circumferences equals the ratio of their radii:
>    $$\frac{C_W}{C_Z} = \frac{2\pi (4)}{2\pi (2)} = \frac{4}{2} = \frac{2}{1}$$
> 3. Therefore, the ratio is **2 to 1**.

</details>

### Question 11
In the $xy$-plane, a quadrilateral has vertices at $(-1, 4)$, $(7, 4)$, $(7, -5)$, and $(-1, -5)$. What is the perimeter of the quadrilateral?
- [A] $17$
- [B] $34$
- [C] $72$
- [D] $80$
- [E] $144$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[B] 34**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. Notice the horizontal and vertical alignments of the vertices:
>    * Top horizontal side from $(-1, 4)$ to $(7, 4)$: Length $= 7 - (-1) = 8$.
>    * Bottom horizontal side from $(-1, -5)$ to $(7, -5)$: Length $= 7 - (-1) = 8$.
>    * Right vertical side from $(7, -5)$ to $(7, 4)$: Length $= 4 - (-5) = 9$.
>    * Left vertical side from $(-1, -5)$ to $(-1, 4)$: Length $= 4 - (-5) = 9$.
> 2. The quadrilateral is a rectangle with width $8$ and height $9$.
> 3. $\text{Perimeter} = 2(\text{width} + \text{height}) = 2(8 + 9) = 2(17) = 34$.

</details>

### Question 15
The random variable $Y$ is normally distributed with mean $500$. The value $340$ is at the 15th percentile of the distribution. Of the following, which is the best estimate of the standard deviation of the distribution?
- [A] $135$
- [B] $145$
- [C] $155$
- [D] $165$
- [E] $175$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[C] 155**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. In a standard normal distribution:
>    * $50\%$ of values lie below the mean ($\mu = 500$).
>    * For a value at the $15\text{th}$ percentile, $15\%$ of values lie below it, meaning $35\%$ lie between the value and the mean ($50\% - 15\% = 35\%$).
> 2. Normal distribution percentile benchmarks:
>    * Recall that $1\sigma$ below the mean captures $34.1\%$ of the distribution, placing $\mu - 1\sigma$ at approximately the $15.9\text{th}$ percentile ($z = -1.00$).
>    * More precisely, for the $15\text{th}$ percentile ($P(Z < z) = 0.15$), the standard normal $z$-score is $z \approx -1.036$.
> 3. Setting up the $z$-score equation:
>    $$z = \frac{Y - \mu}{\sigma} \implies -1.036 = \frac{340 - 500}{\sigma}$$
>    $$-1.036 \sigma = -160 \implies \sigma = \frac{160}{1.036} \approx 154.44$$
> 4. Therefore, the closest and best estimate among the given choices is **$155$**.

</details>

### Question 16
A car dealer received a shipment of cars, half of which were black, with the remainder consisting of equal numbers of blue, silver, and white cars. During the next month, $70$ percent of the black cars, $80$ percent of the blue cars, $30$ percent of the silver cars, and $40$ percent of the white cars were sold. What percent of the cars in the shipment were sold during that month?
- [A] $36\%$
- [B] $50\%$
- [C] $55\%$
- [D] $60\%$
- [E] $72\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[D] 60%**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. Assume a convenient total number of cars in the shipment, say $600$ cars:
>    * Black cars: $\frac{1}{2} \times 600 = 300$ cars.
>    * Remaining cars: $300$ cars, divided equally among blue, silver, and white:
>      * Blue: $100$ cars.
>      * Silver: $100$ cars.
>      * White: $100$ cars.
> 2. Calculate the number of cars sold in each category:
>    * Black sold: $70\% \times 300 = 210$ cars.
>    * Blue sold: $80\% \times 100 = 80$ cars.
>    * Silver sold: $30\% \times 100 = 30$ cars.
>    * White sold: $40\% \times 100 = 40$ cars.
> 3. Total cars sold:
>    $$\text{Total Sold} = 210 + 80 + 30 + 40 = 360 \text{ cars}$$
> 4. Percentage of shipment sold:
>    $$\text{Percent Sold} = \frac{360}{600} \times 100\% = 60\%$$

</details>

### Question 18 *(Select all that apply)*
The distribution of the numbers of hours that students at a certain college studied for final exams has a mean of $12$ hours and a standard deviation of $3$ hours. Which of the following numbers of hours are within $2$ standard deviations of the mean of the distribution?

Indicate all such numbers:
- [A] $5$
- [B] $6$
- [C] $10$
- [D] $13$
- [E] $16$
- [F] $18$
- [G] $19$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[B], [C], [D], [E], and [F]**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**

> 2. A number of hours is within 2 standard deviations if:
>    $$6 \le \text{hours} \le 18$$
> 3. Check choices:
>    * [A] $5 < 6$ (No)

>    * [C] $10$ (Yes)
>    * [D] $13$ (Yes)
>    * [E] $16$ (Yes)

>    * [G] $19 > 18$ (No)

### Question 19 *(Select all that apply)*
In a certain sequence, each term after the first term is obtained by multiplying the preceding term by $2$ and then subtracting $3$ from the product. If the 4th term in the sequence is $19$, which of the following numbers are in the sequence?

Indicate all such numbers:
- [A] $5$
- [B] $7$
- [C] $11$
- [D] $16$
- [E] $22$
- [F] $35$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[A], [B], [C], and [F]**
>
> **Step-by-Step Solution:**
> 1. The recurrence relation is:
>    $$a_n = 2a_{n-1} - 3 \implies a_{n-1} = \frac{a_n + 3}{2}$$
> 2. Given $a_4 = 19$, work backwards to find earlier terms:
>    * $a_3 = \frac{19 + 3}{2} = \frac{22}{2} = 11$
>    * $a_2 = \frac{11 + 3}{2} = \frac{14}{2} = 7$
>    * $a_1 = \frac{7 + 3}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5$
> 3. Work forwards to find subsequent terms:
>    * $a_5 = 2(19) - 3 = 38 - 3 = 35$
>    * $a_6 = 2(35) - 3 = 70 - 3 = 67$
> 4. The sequence terms include: $\{5, 7, 11, 19, 35, 67, \dots\}$.
> 5. Matches among choices: **[A] 5, [B] 7, [C] 11, [F] 35**.

</details>

$$\text{Answer: } \boxed{\phantom{00000000}}$$

### Question 21

> 2. Moving the decimal point 6 places to the right multiplies the number by $10^6$:
>    $$\text{New number} = 10^6 x$$
> 3. Set up the equation:
>    $$10^6 x = 9 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$$
> 4. Multiply both sides by $x$:
>    $$10^6 x^2 = 9 \implies x^2 = \frac{9}{10^6}$$
> 5. Take the positive square root:
>    $$x = \frac{\sqrt{9}}{\sqrt{10^6}} = \frac{3}{10^3} = 0.003$$

</details>
