# AWA 03 – Technology, Efficiency, and Leisure

**“The primary goal of technological advancement should be to increase people's efficiency so that they have more leisure time.”**
> Write a response in which you discuss the extent to which you agree or disagree with the statement and explain your reasoning for the position you take. In developing and supporting your position, you should consider ways in which the statement might or might not hold true and explain how these considerations shape your position.

## Essay

Technological advancement has consistently transformed the way people live, work and interact. From the invention of the printing press to the development of artificial intelligence, technology has often increased efficiency and reduced the time to perform the required tasks. However, to say that the primary goal of technology is to increase people’s efficiency so that they can have more leisure time is a utopian ideal. Agreeably efficiency is an important objective, but it is one of the many desirable outcomes of innovation.

Many of society’s most important technological achievements were developed not to save time but to aid in the betterment of health, safety, and quality of life. The development of mRNA vaccines during the COVID-19 pandemic illustrates the point. Scientists produced effective vaccines in record-breaking time to combat a global health crisis and save millions of lives. Also, the Apollo 11 moon landing required enormous investments of time, money, and effort, yet its purpose was exploration and scientific development rather than convenience. These examples demonstrate that the greatest innovations are often motivated by humanity’s desire to overcome obstacles and push beyond existing limits.

Furthermore, efficiency remains an important objective of technology. Innovations that automate routine tasks can free individuals to pursue education, creativity, and personal interests. If we look at Google’s well-known “20 percent time” policy, which allowed employees to devote part of their workweek to independent projects, directly resulted in groundbreaking innovations like Gmail, and Google Maps. However, this example shows that efficiency should not be viewed as technology’s primary goal. The value of the additional time lay not in the leisure time but in the innovation and creativity it enabled. In other words, efficiency matters as it creates opportunities for higher achievements.

Overall, technological advancements should certainly reduce unnecessary labor and improve efficiency, but these should not be regarded as their central purpose. History shows that the true purpose of technological advancement is to solve increasingly complex human problems and extend the boundaries of what a society can achieve, rather than simply maximizing the time we spend resting.
