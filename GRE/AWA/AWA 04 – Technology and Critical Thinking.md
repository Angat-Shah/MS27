# AWA 04 – Technology and Critical Thinking

**“As people rely more and more on technology to solve problems, the ability of humans to think for themselves will surely deteriorate.”**
> Write a response in which you discuss the extent to which you agree or disagree with the statement and explain your reasoning for the position you take. In developing and supporting your position, you should consider ways in which the statement might or might not hold true and explain how these considerations shape your position.

## Essay

Technology has become an essential part of modern life, helping people solve problems faster and more efficiently than ever before. Some argue that increasing dependence on technology will weaken the thinking ability of humans because people will stop using intelligence for themselves. Although excessive use and dependence on technology can reduce mental skills, to assert that the human thinking ability will surely deteriorate is too extreme. In fact, technology enhances human creativity, decision-making, and problem-solving when used appropriately.

One compelling reason is that technology does not necessarily damage human intelligence but it allows people to focus on rather complex problems. In the past, humans spent a significant amount of their time doing banal, trivial, and repetitive work. Today, computers and artificial intelligence can handle such tasks and allow humans to concentrate on innovation and critical thinking. For instance, the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) uses advanced computer systems to perform complex calculations during space missions such as Chandrayaan. Furthermore, technology provides people with greater access to knowledge, which can actually improve their intellectual abilities. For a student living in the rural areas of India, there is a paucity of access to knowledge but the internet has made it possible for them to study subjects that may not be available in their local schools. Instead of technology reducing their ability to think, it can help effect curiosity and independent learning by exposing individuals to diverse ideas and information.

Nevertheless, technology alone cannot design missions, make strategic decisions, or overcome unexpected challenges. Human creativity and reasoning remain the driving force for such achievements. For example, many students use calculators for mathematics and the usage can go two ways; one if the students use it as an assisted device for trivial tasks and focus on the actual problem then it can be seen as a benefit, but if they simply rely on calculator for all the times and not use their arithmetic skills, then yes their thinking capacity can be reduced. Similarly, in today's time if people heavily rely on artificial intelligence and chatbots, for even a simple decision making then surely it will weaken their critical thinking.

Ultimately, overdependence on technology may create some risks, but it is inaccurate to claim that human ability will inevitably deteriorate. Technology is a tool which can help reduce unnecessary effort and help people solve more important problems. When combined with human judgement and creativity, it strengthens rather than weakens intellectual development.
