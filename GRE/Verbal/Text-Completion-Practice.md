# GRE Verbal Reasoning: Text Completion Practice

Text Completion (TC) questions test your ability to synthesize information, analyze sentence structure, and deduce missing vocabulary based on logical and semantic clues. 

Questions contain **1, 2, or 3 blanks**, with:
- **1-Blank:** 5 choices (select 1)
- **2-Blank:** 3 choices per blank (select 1 for each blank)
- **3-Blank:** 3 choices per blank (select 1 for each blank)

> **Scoring Note:** You must answer every blank correctly to receive credit for the question. There is no partial credit on the GRE.

## Set 1: Discrete Questions (Easy)

### Question 1
Dominant interests often benefit most from ________ of governmental interference in business, since they are able to take care of themselves if left alone.

- [A] intensification
- [B] authorization
- [C] centralization
- [D] improvisation
- [E] elimination

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[E] elimination**
>
> **Explanation:**  
> The clue comes after the comma: "since they are able to take care of themselves if left alone." Being "left alone" means that regulation and oversight are removed. Therefore, dominant entities benefit from the total removal or **elimination** of governmental interference.  
> 
> *Incorrect choices:* **intensification** and **centralization** mean increasing governmental involvement, which contradicts "left alone." **Authorization** (granting official permission) and **improvisation** (making things up on the fly) do not fit the logical requirement.

</details>

### Question 2
Kagan maintains that an infant’s reactions to its first stressful experiences are part of a natural process of development, not harbingers of childhood unhappiness or ________ signs of adolescent anxiety.

- [A] prophetic
- [B] normal
- [C] monotonous
- [D] virtual
- [E] typical

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[A] prophetic**
>
> **Explanation:**  
> The sentence structures two parallel negative clauses: "not [harbingers] of childhood unhappiness or [________] signs of adolescent anxiety." A "harbinger" is an advance omen or foreshadowing of a future event. To preserve parallelism, the blank must mean foretelling or predicting future conditions. **Prophetic** means foretelling or accurately predicting what will happen in the future.  
> 
> *Incorrect choices:* **normal** and **typical** contradict Kagan's argument that these reactions do not indicate ongoing pathologies. **Monotonous** (tedious/repetitive) and **virtual** (simulated/practical) make no sense.

</details>

### Question 3
An investigation that is ________ can occasionally yield new facts, even notable ones, but typically the appearance of such facts is the result of a search in a definite direction.

- [A] timely
- [B] unguided
- [C] consistent
- [D] uncomplicated
- [E] subjective

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[B] unguided**
>
> **Explanation:**  
> The pivot "but typically" sets up a direct contrast: while typical discoveries result from "a search in a definite direction," the opening clause discusses the rare situation where discoveries occur without a focused direction. An investigation lacking a definite direction is **unguided**.  
> 
> *Incorrect choices:* **timely** (well-timed), **consistent** (steady/uniform), **uncomplicated** (simple), and **subjective** (based on personal bias) do not provide the direct contrast to "a search in a definite direction."

</details>

<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

- [A] rationality
- [B] flexibility
- [C] diffidence
- [D] disinterestedness
- [E] insincerity

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

> **Correct Answer:** **[D] disinterestedness**
>
> **Explanation:**  
> The colon introduces an elaboration: "the cynical conviction that everybody has an angle is considered wisdom." If everyone "has an angle" (meaning everyone is motivated by personal bias, profit, or selfish advantage), then pure objectivity and lack of selfish interest must be presumed absent. > *Incorrect choices:* **diffidence** means modesty or shyness. **Rationality** (logic), **flexibility** (adaptability), and **insincerity** (deceitfulness) do not contrast directly with "having an angle."

</details>
